Behind the meter, in plain terms

Power made for your site, delivered on your side of the meter.

“Behind the meter” means the power serving your facility is produced on or next to your site and delivered straight to your load, instead of being pulled from the public grid through a utility connection.

Traditional utility service

  • Power comes from the public grid through the utility’s meter.
  • Large new loads often wait in interconnection queues and for grid upgrades.
  • Your timeline depends on the utility’s timeline.

Behind the meter with GridBridge

  • Generation is built on or next to your site and serves your load directly.
  • Capacity is planned for your campus rather than shared with the grid.
  • The schedule is driven by your project, not a utility queue.

Faster speed to power

Building dedicated supply for your site can avoid long waits for utility interconnection and grid upgrades.

Dedicated capacity

The power system is sized and planned around your load, so your campus isn’t competing for grid capacity.

Power where it’s used

Generation sits close to the load it serves, keeping the path from source to data hall short and under one plan.

Our differentiator

We own the substation.

The substation is where power is switched, protected, and stepped to the voltage your campus needs. GridBridge owns and operates the substation serving your site. That means there’s no hand-off between a power supplier, a substation owner, and a utility: there’s one owner, and it’s us.

Generation → Substation → Your load One accountable owner

  • One accountable owner

    A single point of responsibility from generation through the substation to your load. If something needs attention, you know who to call.

  • Control of interconnection and switching

    Because we own the substation, we control how generation connects to your site and how power is switched and protected.

  • Reliability by design

    The substation and supply are designed together, around the reliability requirements of your facility.

  • Schedule you can plan around

    Owning the critical infrastructure lets us plan, build, and energize on a timeline tied to your project.

How it works

From generation to your data halls, on one path.

Power flows from generation, through the GridBridge-owned substation, directly to your campus.

For landowners & site originators

Turn your site into powered land.

Data center and AI campus developers aren’t just looking for land. They’re looking for land that can get power. A site paired with a committed behind-the-meter power solution and a GridBridge-owned substation can be more valuable and easier to market than raw land waiting on a utility interconnection.

Raw land

  • Power depends on a utility interconnection and its timeline.
  • Buyers have to solve the power question themselves.
  • Harder to position for large-load users.

Powered land with GridBridge

  • A committed behind-the-meter power plan for the site.
  • A GridBridge-owned substation, planned with the site.
  • A clearer story for data center and AI campus buyers.

Capital to build it

Backed by strong capital partners, GridBridge can fund and build the generation and substation for a qualifying site, helping it become powered land. We then own and operate that power infrastructure for the long term.

Partnership structures can be tailored. Depending on the site and your goals, options may include a land lease, a joint development arrangement, or another structure that fits. Not every site will be a fit, and we’ll tell you what we find.
